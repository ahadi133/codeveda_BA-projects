# Predictive, Risk & Strategy Analytics

**Author:** Ahadi Nur  
**Focus:** forecasting outcomes, prioritizing risk, and translating models into recommended action.

This section extends the analysis into predictive and prescriptive work. It demonstrates how model outputs can become clear prioritization rules, scenarios, and decisions rather than isolated accuracy scores.

## Projects

### [Predictive Modeling](predictive_modeling/README.md)

Customer-churn classification, house-price regression, time-series forecasting, and customer segmentation. Includes model comparisons, held-out predictions, forecasts, and a small Streamlit app.

### [Churn Risk Analysis](risk_analysis/README.md)

Converts churn probabilities into low-, medium-, and high-risk cohorts to support prioritization of retention outreach.

### [Prescriptive Strategy Analysis](prescriptive_analytics/README.md)

Compares retention scenarios and produces a recommended allocation under explicit assumptions and capacity constraints.

## Reproducibility

`predictive_modeling.py` and `risk_and_strategy_analysis.py` contain the analysis workflows. Dataset locations are environment-specific and should be updated before rerunning.
