# Churn Risk Analysis

**Author:** Ahadi Nur

## Objective

Convert predictive churn probabilities into clear outreach priorities.

## Approach

Customers are grouped into low, medium, and high risk using transparent probability thresholds. The resulting cohorts support prioritization where retention resources are limited.

## Included Artifacts

- `l3_risk_scores.csv` — risk-band outputs and cohort metrics.
- `l3_risk_charts.png` — visualization of risk segmentation.

## Business Use

High-risk customers can receive proactive outreach, while medium-risk customers can be tested through lower-cost interventions. Risk bands should be monitored and recalibrated as observed churn outcomes accumulate.
