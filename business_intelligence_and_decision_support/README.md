# Business Intelligence & Decision Support

**Author:** Ahadi Nur  
**Focus:** converting data into dashboards, SQL insights, and statistically sound recommendations.

This section brings together reporting, querying, and statistical reasoning around social-media sentiment and customer churn. It is designed to show the practical bridge between analysis and decision-making.

## Projects

### [Social Media Sentiment Reporting](bi_reporting/README.md)

An editable Power BI report and supporting hashtag summary that track sentiment mix, engagement, platform activity, countries, and time-based posting patterns.

### [Customer Churn SQL Analysis](sql_analysis/README.md)

A SQLite analysis of 3,333 customer records, including segmentation, window functions, a regional lookup join, churn drivers, and a query-plan optimization.

### [Customer Churn Statistical Analysis](statistical_analysis/README.md)

Hypothesis tests, confidence intervals, effect sizes, distribution checks, and a proposed A/B-test design to turn churn observations into rigorous business guidance.

## Reproducibility

`business_intelligence_and_decision_support.py` generated the supporting outputs. Update source-dataset paths before executing it in a new environment.
